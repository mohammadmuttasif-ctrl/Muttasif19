Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India

About

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was an Indian aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a key role in India's missile and nuclear programs and was widely known as the "Missile Man of India".

Achievements

He was instrumental in the development of India's satellite launch vehicle and guided missile programs. He received several prestigious awards including the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian honor.

Legacy

Dr. Kalam inspired millions with his vision for a developed India and his dedication to education, innovation, and youth empowerment.
